# Let your notes talk to each other

A good idea rarely lives on a single page. A passage in a book leads to a
question, a question becomes a field note, and that note finds its place
alongside everything you have already read.

## Follow the thread

Keep a [[Reading list|reading list]], collect a few observations, then follow
the links between them. Open related notes in tabs and let one idea lead
naturally to the next.

> [!tip] A library that grows with you
> Start with a folder of Markdown files. Add chapters, images and links as
> your collection grows. Your familiar folders become a comfortable place
> to read, revisit and connect ideas.

## Make room for the important parts

An observation worth keeping: ==reading is a conversation across pages==.
Clear headings, a little emphasis and a well-placed link make it easy to
pick up that conversation tomorrow.
