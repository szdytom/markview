A justified column is one of the harder things a reader can ask of a text
engine: every line has to reach the same right edge without the spaces between
words becoming conspicuous. When the line breaker cannot hyphenate, an ordinary
long word such as "characteristically" or "incomprehensibilities" pushes the
line before it wide open, and the paragraph acquires rivers of white running
down the page.

A web engine sets one line at a time and never reconsiders what it has already
committed to. A paragraph, though, is a single global problem: hyphenating one
word early can rescue a line ten words later. Solving it globally is what keeps
a narrow measure comfortable, and it is the difference a reader feels without
being able to name it.
